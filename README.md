## 🌟 Overview

**Vengeance Player** delivers the best of both worlds: a seamless hybrid platform merging **YouTube Music** and **Spotify** into a single, high-performance Android experience. Enjoy your favorite tracks completely ad-free, with studio-grade audio fidelity and full offline support.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎧 **Spotify & YouTube Music Hybrid:** Stream seamlessly across two of the world's largest music catalogs under one cohesive interface.
- 📥 **Offline Downloads:** Cache and store songs, albums, and playlists locally on your device for offline playback anywhere.
- 🔊 **Audiophile Sound Quality:** High-resolution and lossless audio pipeline (FLAC/Master-quality support) with advanced equalization.
- 🎮 **Discord Rich Presence:** Showcase your current track, artist, album art, and real-time playback progress directly on your Discord profile.
- ✨ **Animated Canvas & Modern UI:** Fluid micro-animations, dynamic canvas backgrounds, and a refined Dark/AMOLED visual aesthetic.
- 🚫 **100% Ad-Free:** Zero audio ads, zero video interruptions, and zero banner ads.
- 🔄 **Built-in GitHub Updates:** Direct in-app update notifications and delivery for the latest Nightly builds.

---

## 🚀 Installation

1. Download the latest `VengeancePlayer.apk` from the Releases section.
2. Open the file on your Android device and confirm installation (enable "Install unknown apps" if prompted).
3. Launch Vengeance Player and start listening.
